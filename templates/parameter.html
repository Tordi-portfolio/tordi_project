Well Log Curves — Engineering Explanation

DEPT – Depth

Represents measured depth of the well (ft or m). All log measurements are plotted against depth.

Logged Interval = Max Depth − Min Depth

DT – Sonic Log (ΔT)

Measures acoustic travel time through formation (μs/ft). Used to estimate porosity.

Φ = (ΔTlog − ΔTmatrix) / (ΔTfluid − ΔTmatrix)

Where:

  • Φ = Porosity
  • ΔTlog = Measured sonic travel time
  • ΔTmatrix = Travel time in rock matrix
  • ΔTfluid = Travel time in fluid

RESD – Deep Resistivity

Measures electrical resistivity of formation (ohm·m). Used to detect hydrocarbons.

Swⁿ = (a × Rw) / (Φᵐ × Rt)

Archie's Equation for water saturation calculation.

SP – Spontaneous Potential

Measures natural electrical potential between borehole and formation (mV).

SSP = −K log10(Rmf / Rw)

Used for lithology identification and estimating formation water resistivity.

GR – Gamma Ray

Measures natural radioactivity (API units). Used to differentiate shale from sand.

IGR = (GRlog − GRmin) / (GRmax − GRmin)

Gamma Ray Index (IGR) used to estimate shale volume (Vsh).

CALI (Caliper)

Measures the actual size and shape of the borehole. If CALI is larger than BITSIZE, the hole is "washed out"; if smaller, a "mudcake" has formed.

BITSIZE

Measures the diameter of the drill bit used to drill that section (e.g. 12.25 inches).

TENS / DHTN (Tension)

The pull on the logging cable. TENS is usually surface tension, while DHTN is downhole tension. High tension can indicate the tool is getting stuck.

RHOB (Bulk Density)

Measures the density of the formation. Used to calculate porosity and identify lithology.

NPHI (Neutron Porosity)

Measures the hydrogen index. When paired with RHOB, it helps identify gas zones (the "crossover" effect) and fluid types.

PE (Photoelectric Index)

Directly indicates the mineralogy of the rock matrix (e.g., ~1.8 for sandstone, ~3.1 for shales, ~5.0 for limestone).

MDT (Acoustic/Sonic)

These measure the travel time of sound through the rock. MDT (MonoDeltaT) is used to calculate "Sonic Porosity."

RT10, RT20, RT30

"Shallow" measurements that look at the area near the wellbore (invaded zone).
