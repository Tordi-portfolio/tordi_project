📘 About Water Saturation

Water saturation (Sw) is the fraction of pore space in a reservoir rock that is filled with water. It is critical for estimating hydrocarbon volume.

📐 Archie’s Equation

Sw = [ (a * Rw) / (φ^m * Rt) ]^(1/n)

📊 Units

  • Rw (Formation water resistivity): ohm·m
  • Rt (True formation resistivity): ohm·m
  • φ (Porosity): fraction (NOT %)
  • a, m, n: dimensionless constants

⚠️ How to Use

Convert porosity from % to fraction (e.g. 20% → 0.20). Ensure resistivity values are in ohm·m. Typical values: a = 1, m = 2, n = 2.

📊 Relationships Between Parameters

Rt ↑ → Sw ↓

As true formation resistivity increases, water saturation decreases. High resistivity usually indicates hydrocarbon presence because oil and gas resist electrical flow.

φ ↑ → Sw ↓

Higher porosity means more pore space available for hydrocarbons, leading to lower water saturation in most reservoir conditions.

Rw ↑ → Sw ↑

Higher formation water resistivity increases calculated water saturation. This often occurs in fresh water systems with lower ionic content.

m ↑ → Sw ↓

Increasing cementation exponent affects pore geometry, making the rock matrix more resistive and influencing saturation estimates.

n controls Sw sensitivity

The saturation exponent controls how strongly resistivity responds to changes in water saturation.

a ↑ → Sw ↑

Tortuosity factor represents pore path complexity. More complex pathways increase resistance and affect saturation calculations.


Archie’s Equation
Sw = [(a × Rw) / (φ^m × Rt)]^(1/n)

Used to calculate water saturation in clean sandstone reservoirs with no shale content.

Parameters: Sw, a, Rw, φ, m, Rt, n

Formation Factor
F = Rt / Rw = a / φ^m

Used to relate rock resistivity to porosity in clean formations.

Parameters: F, Rt, Rw, a, φ, m

Resistivity Index
Sw = (R0 / Rt)^(1/n)

Used to estimate water saturation from resistivity ratio of water-saturated and hydrocarbon zones.

Parameters: Sw, R0, Rt, n

Simandoux Model
1/Rt = (Vsh / Rsh) + (Sw^n / (a × Rw × φ^m))

Used in shaly sand formations to account for shale conductivity.

Parameters: Rt, Vsh, Rsh, Sw, a, Rw, φ, m, n

Indonesia Equation
Sw = [ (Vsh / Rsh)^(1/2) + (φ^m / (a × Rw × Rt))^(1/2) ]^2

Used for water saturation estimation in complex shaly sand reservoirs.

Parameters: Sw, Vsh, Rsh, φ, m, a, Rw, Rt

Waxman–Smits Model
Sw = [F (Rw + BQv) / Rt]^(1/n)

Used when clay conductivity significantly affects formation resistivity.

Parameters: Sw, F, Rw, B, Qv, Rt, n
