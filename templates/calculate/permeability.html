📘 About Permeability

Permeability (k) describes how easily fluids flow through reservoir rock. It is one of the most important parameters in reservoir engineering and production forecasting.

📐 Empirical Formula

k = C × φ⁴ / Sw²

📐 Darcy Core Flood Formula

k = (Q × μ × L) / (A × ΔP)

📊 Units

  • k: Darcy / mD
  • Q: cm³/s
  • μ: cP
  • L: cm
  • A: cm²
  • ΔP: atm or bar

⚠️ Engineering Note

Always ensure consistent units before solving. Darcy method is lab-based while empirical method is log-based estimation.
