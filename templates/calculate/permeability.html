📘 About Permeability

Permeability (k) measures the ability of a rock to transmit fluids through its pore network. It is a key parameter in reservoir productivity analysis.

📐 Empirical Formula

k = C × φ⁴ / Sw²

📐 Darcy Core Flood Formula

k = (Q × μ × L) / (A × ΔP)

📊 Units

  • k: Darcy or milliDarcy (mD)
  • Q: cm³/s
  • μ (viscosity): cP
  • L: cm
  • A: cm²
  • ΔP: atm or bar (be consistent)

⚠️ How to Use

Ensure consistent units before calculation. Core flood method is used for lab data, while empirical method is used for log-based estimation.
