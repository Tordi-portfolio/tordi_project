Empirical Formula
k = C · φ⁴ / Swi²
k = Permeability (Darcy)  |  C = Empirical constant  |  φ = Porosity (fraction)  |  Swi = Irreducible water saturation
Used when only porosity and connate water saturation data are available. Higher porosity and lower Swi give higher permeability.

Darcy's Law — Core Plug Method
k = (Q · μ · L) / (A · ΔP)
k = Permeability (Darcy)  |  Q = Flow rate (cc/s)  |  μ = Viscosity (cP)  |  L = Core length (cm)
A = Cross-sectional area = π·d²/4 (cm²)  |  ΔP = Pressure drop (atm)
Used on laboratory core plugs. Directly measures fluid flow capacity.
