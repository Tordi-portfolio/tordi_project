Archie Equation
Archie's Equation
Sw = ((a · Rw) / (Rt · φm))1/n
Sw = Water saturation (fraction)  |  Rw = Resistivity of formation water  |  Rt = True resistivity of the formation
φ = Porosity (fraction)  |  a = Tortuosity factor  |  m = Cementation exponent  |  n = Saturation exponent
Lower Sw combined with good porosity generally points to greater hydrocarbon saturation.
