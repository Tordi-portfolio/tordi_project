Density Porosity Formula
φ = (ρma − ρb) / (ρma − ρf)
φ = Porosity (fraction)  |  ρma = Matrix density (g/cm³)  |  ρb = Bulk density (g/cm³)  |  ρf = Fluid density (g/cm³)
Derived from the density log. Higher bulk density relative to matrix density indicates lower porosity.
